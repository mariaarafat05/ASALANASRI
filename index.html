I'M ASALA NASRI

I'M A SINGER

I was born in Damascus to a Damascene family and grew up there,
specifically in the Abu Rummaneh neighborhood.
I entered the singing field in 2005,
And I have many songs and albums to my credit.
Asala Nasri (May 15, 1969) is a Syrian singer, considered
one of the most popular and famous Arab singers.
